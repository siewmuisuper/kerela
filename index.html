02Fri, 25 Dec

Kochi → Munnar (4–5 hrs)

Stay: Fog Resort

Depart around 8.30–9.00am after breakfast. Scenic roadside stops at waterfalls and tea plantations on the climb up. Check in, relax, and enjoy a Christmas dinner at the resort.

Venues along the way

07Wed, 30 Dec

Kumarakom → Kochi Drive ≈1.5–2 hrs · Fort Kochi ≈30 min from hotel

Stay: Olive Downtown — 30 Dec to 1 Jan (confirmed) · Kochi town, Kadavanthra

Optional morning backwater cruise before checkout. Check in at Olive Downtown, then make the ≈30-minute trip to Fort Kochi to explore the old quarter — Chinese Fishing Nets, Princess Street, Jew Town — ending with a sunset walk by the waterfront.

Worth knowingOlive Downtown is in Kochi town (Kadavanthra), about 30 minutes from Fort Kochi. Allow time for the return trip after the sunset walk. The hotel stay runs 30 Dec to 1 Jan, so there is no hotel change on 31 Dec.

08Thu, 31 Dec

Kochi Fort Kochi ≈30 min from hotel

Stay: Olive Downtown (AC room) — same hotel, no move today

Relaxed morning. Optional shopping at Lulu Mall or MG Road. New Year's Eve dinner or celebration before the final night.

No entrance fees — a shopping and dining day.

FoodNew Year's Eve dinner — Fort Kochi has good options for a celebratory meal (seafood grills, rooftop restaurants), a short taxi ride from Olive Downtown (≈30 min journey).
Worth knowingOlive Downtown is convenient for Lulu Mall/MG Road and Kadavanthra metro. For a NYE dinner in Fort Kochi, plan the ≈30 min journey each way and arrange the ride back in advance, as taxis are in high demand on New Year's Eve.
